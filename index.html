Material Integrity

From High Highland Pastures to Woven Heirloom

In the valleys of Gilgit, Hunza, and Skardu, sheep wool has shielded mountain households for generations. ReGongches works directly with village weavers who spin hand-washed highland fleece on wooden drop-spindles, preserving natural lanolin for water and chill resistance.

01

Hand-Spun Sheep Wool

Naturally washed and spun on family wooden spindles without industrial harsh scouring agents.

02

Pit-Loom Patti Weaving

Dense herringbone and twill weaves constructed on traditional pit looms for longevity.

03

Silk Needlework Motifs

Geometric mountain symbols hand-stitched thread-by-thread in pure silk floss without printed tracings.
