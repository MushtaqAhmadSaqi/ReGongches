Apparel Measurement Standard

ReGongches Size & Fit Guide (cm & inches)

Head Circumference: 56 cm (22.0 in) → Recommended ReGongches Size: Medium (M — 56 cm)

How to measure your head for a Gilgiti or Hunza Cap: Wrap a flexible tape measure around your head 1.5 cm (0.6 in) above your ears and across the middle of your forehead. Hold comfortably snug.

Label Size Head Circumference (cm) Head Circumference (inches) Fit Recommendation
Small (S) 54 cm 21.2 in Snug crown; youths or smaller adult head
Medium (M) 56 cm 22.0 in Most common standard adult fit
Large (L) 58 cm 22.8 in Relaxed fit or thicker hair
Extra Large (XL) 60 cm 23.6 in Generous crown circumference

How to measure for a Pattu Waistcoat or Jacket: Measure around the fullest part of your chest over a light kameez or shirt. Our waistcoats include a 4 cm (1.5 in) comfort ease.

Artisan Craft Process

From Wool to Wear

01

Shearing & Spinning

Hand-washed highland wool spun on wooden drop-spindles, preserving natural lanolin for water and chill resistance.

02

Loom Weaving

Woven on traditional pit looms into dense, wind-defying wool patti and herringbone twills with uniform hand-tension.

03

Silk Needlework

Counted-thread geometric mountain motifs hand-stitched in pure silk floss without printed stencils or tracings.

04

Origin Certification

Measured in exact centimetres, quality-checked, and paired with a physical, wax-sealed artisan Origin Card.
